# Ruleset is bespoke, Shadowrun-derived

We keep Shadowrun's fingerprint mechanics — a d6 pool of attribute + skill where 5s and 6s are Hits, Glitches, Edge as a small luck pool, two Condition Monitors with Stun-to-Physical Overflow, and initiative expressed as an Energy budget — and redesign everything else (skills, classes, magic, hacking, progression) as our own system. Limits are dropped (SR6 dropped them too), and so is the tabletop's opposed-chain depth: no test in this game is longer than two rolls.

**Considered options**: a faithful SR5 subset (rejected: Limits, astral space, and extended tests are tabletop bookkeeping that buys nothing on a grid); an abstract flavor-only system with one roll vs a target number (rejected: throws away the arithmetic texture the design is built around); our own math from scratch (rejected: then it is a generic cyberpunk RPG wearing Shadowrun nouns, and there is no reason to keep the noun).

**Consequences**: we inherit Shadowrun's dice-pool feel without inheriting its bookkeeping, and every rule we keep must justify itself in a combat log a player reads.
