# Fixed crew of four; Physical Adept replaces Street Samurai; no Essence

The playable roster is exactly four — Physical Adept, Mage, Shaman, Decker — and all four exist from character creation, with no recruitment and no class selection moment. The Street Samurai is not playable: the physical class is a Physical Adept with prodigal attributes and a Qi pool, so chrome is not the route to physical power and there is no Essence economy to price it. Cyberware still exists in the world as NPC and enemy equipment.

**Considered options**: a Street Samurai with chrome limited by nuyen and hardware slots (rejected: it reintroduces a shopping loop as a class identity); build-your-protagonist-then-recruit (rejected for v1: it front-loads a recruitment and personality system before the core loop works; deeper narrative later may reopen it).

**Consequences**: no Essence means no "chrome costs your humanity" pressure and no reason to track implant grades. The Adept's identity is carried entirely by Qi powers, so those powers are load-bearing rather than flavour.
