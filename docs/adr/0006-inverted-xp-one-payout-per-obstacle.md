# Inverted XP, one failure payout per obstacle

Failure is the fastest route to power: a successful test pays 1 XP, a failed test pays 5 XP and rolls a random Perk from a table. To stop infinite retry farming, each obstacle pays its failure XP exactly once — a given lock, conversation, or firefight has one payout no matter how many times it is failed.

**Considered options**: risk-roll gating, where only rolls with a stated consequence pay (rejected as the primary rule: it needs a reliable notion of "consequence" that content authors will get wrong, though it remains the tuning knob if farming appears); a per-run failure cap (rejected: arbitrary ceiling, and farming to the cap is still optimal); allowing farming and punishing it purely with Clock escalation (rejected as the *only* defence: it makes progression balance hostage to pacing balance).

**Consequences**: the known hole is farming across many *trivial* obstacles — each pays once, but a Site full of easy locks is a Site full of XP. Mitigation is content-side (obstacles that pay must be scene-significant) and the risk-roll rule is the documented fallback. Random Perks may push a Runner away from their archetype, which is accepted as the price of failure-driven growth.
